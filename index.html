ABOUT THE PROJECT

Travel planning that brings everyone along.

Trip Companion — Tour Together & Expense Tracker is a student project designed to make Karnataka travel planning clearer, more organised, and budget-aware.

What can you do?

Explore: browse landmarks, temples, nature spots, and stay suggestions across all 31 districts.

Plan: build a starter itinerary for one day, two days, or longer and choose solo, couple, family, or friends travel.

Organise: save trips, keep travel goals, and submit a demo booking request.

Track: record food, transport, hotel, ticket, and other costs in one expense list.

Built with Python, Flask, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SQLite. This is an educational demo, not a live booking provider.
